### Highlights

- **MeoCord 4.1 adds to 4.0.** Upgrading from 4.0, below, lists what a 4.0 bot may do differently or what may now stop it, and [What's new in 4.1](https://meocord.dev/docs/4.1/whats-new) walks through every addition.
- **One way to answer Discord.** `respond(interaction)` acknowledges, sends, follows up and reports errors in whatever form the interaction allows, `@Defer()` acknowledges before slow work, and a presenter decides how MeoCord's own loading, error and help answers look, in colours and emojis a theme names. See [Responses](https://meocord.dev/docs/4.1/responses) and [Theming](https://meocord.dev/docs/4.1/theming).
- **A pipeline around every handler.** Global and per-handler guards, interceptors, exception filters, validation pipes, `@Cooldown` and observers run in one fixed order, each around the handlers it applies to, and `ExecutionContext` with `createMetadata` gives each stage typed facts about the handler. See [How a call runs](https://meocord.dev/docs/4.1/how-a-call-runs).
- **Message commands with patterns.** `@MessageHandler('roll {sides:int}')` takes typed params, flags and aliases after a prefix or a mention, answers a message that doesn't fit with its usage, and the app can have a built-in `!help`. See [Message commands](https://meocord.dev/docs/4.1/message-commands).
- **Sharding, providers and lifecycle hooks.** A bot runs sharded in one process or a process per shard, with `ShardContext.call` to reach every shard; providers supply values, classes and async factories; `onReady` and `onShutdown` run in dependency order, and `app.stop()` shuts a bot down from code. See [Sharding](https://meocord.dev/docs/4.1/sharding) and [Lifecycle hooks](https://meocord.dev/docs/4.1/lifecycle-hooks).
- **Localisation and a testing toolkit.** Typed catalogs translate commands, replies and MeoCord's own texts, and `MeoCordTestingModule` runs a handler through everything the bot runs around it, with mocks shaped like Discord's data. See [Localisation](https://meocord.dev/docs/4.1/localisation) and [Testing](https://meocord.dev/docs/4.1/testing).
