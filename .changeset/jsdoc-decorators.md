The JSDoc of every decorator in `meocord/decorator`, and of `CooldownOptions`, `DeferOptions` and `CommandBuilderOptions`, is rewritten for the hover in your editor: a one-line summary, when to use it and what to use instead, how it works, where it runs in a call, and an example that compiles against the published types. The options of `@Guard`, `@Interceptor`, `@Observer`, `@Validate`, `@Cooldown` and `@Defer` are documented on each option, with its default.
