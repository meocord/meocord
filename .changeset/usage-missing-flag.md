A message command's usage reply names a required flag the message left out beside what its words lack, in one reply. For `pin {text...} {--in:channel}`, `!pin` was told only `text is missing`, and `--in is missing` once the words fitted; it is now told both. The reply uses MeoCord's existing texts, so a catalog that translates them translates it too.
