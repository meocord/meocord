A testing module closed during `init({ ready: true })` now runs the `onReady` and `onShutdown` hooks as the bot does when it stops before or during its ready hooks. Once `init({ ready: true })` is asked, `close()` lets no further `onReady` start, and runs no `onShutdown` for a unit whose `onReady` hasn't settled when `close()` lists what it shuts down, such as one still running or one that never started because `close()` came first, nor for anything that depends on it. A unit whose `onReady` failed has settled and is shut down. Before, the module went on with the `onReady` hooks after `close()`. Once `close()` stopped waiting for them, at its `shutdownTimeout` less the hooks' reserve, it ran `onShutdown` for every unit it had made, one still in its `onReady` included, and the units the later hooks made had nothing to shut them down. A `close()` before the hooks started, while `init()` made the providers, waited for every factory, then did the same. A test that calls `close()` before the `onReady` hooks finish now sees the bot's shutdown: if it expected the later hooks to run, or a unit whose `onReady` hasn't settled to be shut down, await `init({ ready: true })` before `close()`. Without `init({ ready: true })`, nothing changes: `close()` still shuts down every unit the module made. See https://meocord.dev/docs/4.3/testing.
